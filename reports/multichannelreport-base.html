Why CatholicAdNet Web Display Should Work Alongside Facebook

If your organization already advertises on Facebook, CatholicAdNet web display should not be evaluated as a competing replacement. The two channels reach people in different environments, perform different jobs and can make one another more efficient when they reinforce the same campaign.

The central idea

  • Facebook is strong at discovery, engagement and audience building.
  • CatholicAdNet extends your campaign across trusted Catholic websites.
  • Repeated exposure across channels can improve recall and increase later search, direct and email conversions.
  • The appropriate test is whether the combined mix lowers acquisition cost—including Facebook CPA—and reduces overall blended CPA compared with running Facebook in isolation.

What you will learn

  • Where Facebook, web display, search and email contribute in the journey
  • Why web display is often undervalued by last-click attribution
  • How coordinated channels can lower blended acquisition cost
  • What independent research says about cross-channel reach and performance

Your customers rarely move from first exposure to conversion in a single step. Facebook may create interest, CatholicAdNet may reinforce the message on trusted Catholic sites, search may capture active intent and email may provide the final reminder.

Facebook’s role

  • Create discovery and engagement inside Facebook and Instagram
  • Build remarketing audiences and generate qualified site visits
  • Use video, social proof and creative variation to develop interest
  • Capture immediate responses from people ready to act

CatholicAdNet’s role

  • Extend your reach across trusted Catholic websites
  • Reinforce your message after a person leaves social media
  • Maintain visibility during a longer consideration period
  • Support retargeting, return visits and assisted conversions
The goal of a multichannel approach is to lower acquisition cost by making the channels work better together than they do alone. In a siloed Facebook campaign, Facebook CPA itself may be higher because the platform must create, reinforce and capture demand without support from CatholicAdNet web display.

Evaluate Facebook on discovery, engagement and qualified visits; CatholicAdNet on incremental reach, frequency, assisted conversions and return visits; Search on intent capture; and Email on nurture, revenue per recipient and retention.

Why CatholicAdNet web display complements Facebook

When Facebook is already creating interest, CatholicAdNet can carry the campaign into other trusted environments. This increases the number of opportunities for your audience to recognize, remember and revisit your offer.

Three ways the channels reinforce each other

1

Extend reach

Facebook reaches people inside its platform. CatholicAdNet adds exposure across Catholic news, ministry and content sites.

2

Reinforce memory

Seeing a consistent message in more than one environment can improve recognition and increase the chance of a later branded search or direct visit.

3

Recapture attention

Web display can remind social engagers and previous site visitors about an offer after the initial Facebook interaction has passed.

A practical way to think about the mix

Facebook helps your organization create and qualify interest. CatholicAdNet helps you extend that interest across the Catholic web, reinforce the message and bring people back when they are ready to act.

The right question is not: “Does CatholicAdNet beat Facebook on last-click CPA?”

The right question is: “Does adding CatholicAdNet help Facebook convert more efficiently and produce a lower blended CPA than a Facebook-only campaign?”

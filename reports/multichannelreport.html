How Multichannel Marketing Can Lower Blended CPA — and Why Display Matters in the Mix

This report is designed for client conversations. It explains why channels should be judged by their role in the funnel, why display is often a powerful complement to Facebook and other channels, and how a coordinated media mix can lower blended CPA when incremental conversions rise faster than total acquisition spend.

Core argument for clients

  • No single channel wins alone. Different channels do different jobs.
  • Display is usually not the last click — but it often helps create or reinforce the conversions other channels capture.
  • Facebook and paid social excel at discovery and engagement. Display extends reach across the open web and supports repeated exposure.
  • Blended CPA falls when incremental conversion growth outpaces acquisition spend growth.

Why display is a strong complement to Facebook and other channels

This section is intentionally geared to sales conversations where you need to position display as a legitimate contributor to performance, not merely a branding add-on.

Strategic role of display

Display helps fill the gaps between discovery, evaluation and conversion. It is particularly useful when Facebook is already creating interest, because display can continue the story after the user leaves the platform.

1

Extend reach

Facebook reaches audiences inside the platform. Display extends your presence across the open web and adds efficient incremental reach.

2

Reinforce memory

Repeated exposure across environments can improve brand recall, message retention and the chance of a later branded search or direct visit.

3

Recapture attention

Display retargeting can bring back people who engaged with social, visited the site or started but did not complete a conversion action.

Suggested client phrasing

“Facebook helps us create and qualify interest. Display helps us carry that interest across the broader web, reinforce the message and bring people back when they are ready to act.”

Important: The right question is not “Does display beat Facebook on last-click CPA?” The right question is “Does the combination produce more total incremental conversions and a better blended CPA than Facebook alone?”
